## FONT_MAP Small Caps
- Added Unicode small caps FONT_MAP (A-Z, a-z → ᴀ-ᴢ)
- `toSmallCaps(text)` — converts any text to small caps
- `sc(text)` — shortcut for small caps
- `scBold(text)` — bold + small caps
- `sendScMessage(chat, text)` — sends message with small caps

## Small Caps Applied To:
- Welcome message buttons and text
- Control menu buttons and text
- Photo/video action messages ("Photo nicchi…")
- Scroll/home/back/recents feedback
- App launch messages
- Back to welcome text
- All user-facing callback responses

## NOT Small Caps:
- /help command list (stays normal for readability)
- RichMessage HTML (uses its own formatting)
- Internal error messages
